Our Subteams

01 // Hardware

Building, assembling, and wiring our competition robot—from drivetrain construction and linear slides to intake mechanisms, cable management, and pit repairs.

02 // CAD & Design

Modeling every mechanism in 3D before a single part is cut, running tolerance checks inside the 18" sizing cube, and fabricating custom 3D-printed and CNC parts.

03 // Software

Programming precision autonomous routines with Road Runner/Pedro Pathing, odometry localization, AprilTag computer vision, and TeleOp controls in Java.

04 // Outreach & Portfolio

Documenting our engineering journey for the Inspire Award, connecting with local sponsors, and hosting hands-on STEM workshops for students in our community.
